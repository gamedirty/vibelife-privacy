Key Features

  • Live Decibel Readout (dB): Crisp, smooth real-time sound level measurement with instant noise zone badge.
  • Expressive Cat Companion: Visual noise feedback at a glance — ideal for study rooms, classrooms, nurseries, and offices.
  • MIN / AVG / PEAK Stats: Track minimum, average, and peak decibels throughout your session.
  • 30-Second Acoustic Waveform: Watch noise spikes and quiet dips roll across a live studio chart.
  • Pause, Hold & Reset: Freeze a reading anytime or reset session stats with a single tap.
  • Built-In Noise Reference Guide: Quickly compare everyday environments from whisper (20 dB) to concert (105+ dB).
  • 12 Languages Supported: English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Russian, Indonesian, and Hindi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does HushMeter record or upload microphone audio?

No. Microphone input is processed strictly on your Android device in real time to calculate sound pressure levels (dB) and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted.

Is HushMeter free to use?

Yes, HushMeter is 100% free on Google Play with zero sign-up required.

What decibel level wakes Mochi the cat?

Mochi naps calmly below 32 dB, reacts as room volume rises, and covers his studio headphones above 82 dB to warn about loud noise.
