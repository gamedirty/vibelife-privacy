Frequently Asked Questions

Is CalcWave a real scientific calculator?

Yes! CalcWave includes a full 28-operation scientific engine with sin/cos/tan & inverses, DEG/RAD toggle, ln/log/exp/10^x, roots, powers, factorial, mod, π, e, Ans, and 30-item calculation history.

What skins are included in CalcWave?

CalcWave includes 4 full-body mechanical keycap colorways — Classic Braun, Uji Matcha Cream, Sakura Candy Pop, and Midnight Cyber — all free to switch anytime.

Can I turn off the mechanical key click sound?

Yes, tap the speaker icon in the top bar anytime to toggle the mechanical key-click audio on or off.
