BeamLite: Flashlight & SOS

Tactile slide-switch LED flashlight with SOS, strobe, campfire & aurora lights.

Key Features

  • Tactile Mechanical Slide-Switch: Drag up/down or tap to toggle bright LED torch with crisp detent haptics.
  • 3 Tactical Torch Modes: Steady high-output beam, international Morse-code SOS emergency beacon, and speed-adjustable Strobe (Hz frequency slider).
  • 4 Live 60fps Mood & Safety Screen Lights:
  • Works Even Without LED Flash: Automatic fallback to full-brightness screen lighting.
  • 12 Languages Supported: Clean, lightweight, zero login required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does BeamLite work on phones without a camera LED flash?

Yes! In addition to high-output LED torch control, BeamLite includes 4 full-screen 60fps screen lights (Campfire, Aurora, Beacon, and Bright daylight white).

Can I adjust the strobe flash speed?

Yes, switch to Strobe mode and drag the tactile frequency slider to dial in the exact flash rate in Hz.

Does BeamLite require camera photo storage permissions?

No. BeamLite uses Android's CameraManager torch API without capturing or storing any photos or video.
