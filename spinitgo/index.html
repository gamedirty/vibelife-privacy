Key Features

  • Direct Finger-Flick Physics: Drag and flick the wheel with your thumb in either direction with smooth realistic deceleration and tactile haptic clicks.
  • Illuminated Carnival Stage: 24 chasing champagne-gold rim bulbs, jewel-toned wedges, glowing winner card, and celebratory confetti burst.
  • 4 Built-In Quick Presets: Dinner, Yes / No, Movie Night, and Weekend Plans — localized and ready immediately.
  • Custom Choice Editor: Easily add or remove up to 10 custom options for raffles, dares, study topics, or party games.
  • 12 Languages Supported: Instant fun for friends, couples, families, and classrooms — no sign-up required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I physically flick the wheel with my finger?

Yes! SpinItGo uses GPU-accelerated rigid-body physics so you can grab and flick the wheel clockwise or counter-clockwise — or tap SPIN.

Can I create my own custom options?

Yes, you can choose from 4 built-in presets (Dinner, Yes/No, Movie, Weekend) or customize up to 10 of your own choices.

How many languages does SpinItGo support?

SpinItGo supports 12 languages natively, including localized preset wheels.
