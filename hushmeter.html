Privacy Policy

App: HushMeter: Sound & dB Meter (com.quietline.hushmeter) · Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how HushMeter: Sound & dB Meter handles information when you use our mobile application. We design our utility tools to work offline and locally on your device without requiring an account or sign-in.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

App settings, preferences, custom lists, or session history are saved solely in local on-device storage (Android SharedPreferences) and are never uploaded to our servers. You can clear all local data at any time through your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Device Permissions

Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) is used strictly on-device in real time to measure ambient sound pressure level (dB). Audio is NEVER recorded, saved, or transmitted off your device.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

This app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location, app interaction events, and crash/diagnostic data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset or limit personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
